Alla AI — Tutoring

What Alla AI is for

Math is the main focus: start with the foundations, then build understanding through a worked example and guided practice. That means refreshing single-digit multiplication before harder products or area. Alla AI can also help with other academic subjects.

Use it to learn and practice, not to copy answers or do graded work for you. Try each problem, check your understanding, and ask for another explanation whenever a step is unclear.

Mission Statement

Alla AI was made to make learning more approachable and accessible. We want students to have a useful alternative to copying answers: an interactive tutor that explains ideas and gives them a chance to practice.

About the Primary Creator

Benjamin Concannon (Allamein) is a young creator/developer who lives in Massachusetts. Since June 26th, 2024, he has been making small projects. Alla AI was made from personal experience; Benjamin saw people not using AI for studying, and he wanted to change it.
