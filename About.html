Alla AI: About

Mission Statement

Alla AI was made for one simple goal: to actually help real people. We believe that if you make learning easy and accessible, then people will be able to learn. Alla AI is made to stop people cheating on tests by giving them an easy alternative in the form of a tutor giving practice problems.

About the Primary Creator

Benjamin Concannon (Allamein) is a young creator/developer who lives in Massachusetts. Since June 26th, 2024, he has been making small projects. Alla AI was made from personal experience; Benjamin saw people not using AI for studying, and he wanted to change it.
